If by "survive in the future" you mean skills that are likely to remain valuable despite AI, automation, and rapid technological change, these are among the strongest candidates:

## 1. Critical Thinking

The ability to evaluate information, identify flaws in reasoning, and make sound decisions. As AI generates more content, distinguishing truth from noise becomes increasingly important.

## 2. Learning How to Learn

People who can quickly acquire new skills will adapt better than those relying on a fixed set of knowledge.

## 3. Communication

Writing, speaking, presenting, and explaining complex ideas clearly will remain essential in nearly every profession.

## 4. Problem Solving

Employers and organizations value people who can tackle unfamiliar challenges rather than simply follow procedures.

## 5. Emotional Intelligence

Understanding emotions, building relationships, resolving conflicts, and collaborating effectively are difficult to automate.

## 6. Digital & AI Literacy

Knowing how to use AI tools, evaluate AI outputs, automate workflows, and work alongside technology will become a basic professional requirement.

## 7. Creativity

Generating original ideas, designing solutions, storytelling, innovation, and combining concepts in new ways are areas where humans still have strong advantages.

## 8. Adaptability

Industries, tools, and careers will continue changing rapidly. Being comfortable with uncertainty and change is a major competitive advantage.

## 9. Systems Thinking

Understanding how different parts of a business, society, technology stack, or ecosystem interact helps solve complex problems that AI alone cannot fully address.

## 10. Leadership & Influence

Motivating people, setting direction, negotiating, building trust, and making difficult decisions are enduring human skills.

### If you're a student today

A particularly powerful combination is:

1. AI literacy
2. Communication
3. Critical thinking
4. Problem solving
5. Emotional intelligence

These five together can make you effective in almost any future career.

### Skills most likely to lose value

- Routine data entry
- Basic administrative work
- Simple content creation
- Repetitive coding tasks
- Work that follows fixed rules without judgment

The future will likely reward people who can **use AI effectively, think independently, communicate clearly, and work well with other humans**. Those capabilities are much harder to automate than routine tasks.
