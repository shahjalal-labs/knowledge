### What should we ask every wholesaler?

Ask the same questions everywhere:

> 1. What are your best-selling products?
> 2. Which colors sell fastest?
> 3. Which sizes move fastest?
> 4. What is your MOQ?
> 5. Do you offer a better price for repeat buyers?
> 6. Can defective items be exchanged?
> 7. When do you receive new stock?
> 8. Can I order later through WhatsApp or courier?
